About Peacy Games

Peacy Games is an independent game development studio focused on creating distinctive interactive experiences across PC, Android, VR, and console platforms.

Our portfolio includes Demon Alive, Tig Tag Flag, Up Break, Hezarfen, Mahjong Sights, and more. This website is the official home of Peacy Games and links directly to our verified publisher and developer profiles.
