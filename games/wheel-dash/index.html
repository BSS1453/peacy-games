About Wheel Dash

Short description

Dodge road hazards, collect cars and challenge a rival in top-down arcade races.

Detailed description

Hold to accelerate, slide to steer and release to stop. Rolling tires keep moving, so timing matters even at a standstill.

Chase distance records in endless solo runs or join a two-player online race to a 1,500-meter finish. Build a 60-car garage with different driving stats.

Use healing, shields, magnets and nitro through changing scenery. Daily rewards, missions and leaderboards offer more goals. The game includes ads and optional purchases, including premium cars with gameplay advantages. Online features, purchases and ads require internet access.
