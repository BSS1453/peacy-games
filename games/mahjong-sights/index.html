About Mahjong Sights

Short description

Match world-famous landmarks in travel-themed Mahjong boards with hidden tiles, guaranteed-solvable layouts and a special quick-win pair.

Detailed description

Mahjong Sights turns tile matching into a sightseeing journey. Boards use famous landmarks and travel imagery, and the core objective is to clear matching pairs while moving through locations inspired by places around the world.

Some tiles begin face-down and are revealed only after the surrounding pieces have been cleared. That adds an extra layer of planning because useful pairs may not be visible at the start. A special quick-win pair can also end a level immediately when both parts are uncovered and matched.

Boards are generated to remain solvable, so the challenge comes from your decisions rather than an impossible layout. Visual feedback and particle effects support the relaxed presentation, and Mahjong Sights works for both online and offline sessions.

Mahjong Sights FAQ

What makes Mahjong Sights different?

Its boards use world landmarks and add hidden tiles plus a special quick-win pair to classic matching.

Are the levels intended to be solvable?

Yes. Each generated board is designed to be solvable from the start.

Can Mahjong Sights be played offline?

Yes. Mahjong Sights supports both offline and online play.
