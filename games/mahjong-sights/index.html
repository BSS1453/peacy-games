About Mahjong Sights

Mahjong Sights turns tile matching into a world tour by using famous landmarks and travel motifs throughout its boards. Standard pair matching is combined with hidden face-down tiles that must be uncovered by clearing surrounding pieces. Some levels also contain a special quick-win pair that can finish the board immediately when matched. The Google Play listing states that boards are generated to be solvable from the start, with play designed for both short breaks and longer puzzle sessions, online or offline.

Gameplay information is based on the current official Google Play listing.
