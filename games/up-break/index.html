About Up Break

Up Break is a fast arcade game built around a rocket-drill that tears through stacks of colorful platforms. Every platform adds to your score, while levels reward up to three stars and encourage you to improve your personal best. Progress also earns coins that can be spent on country-flag characters, and global leaderboards let you compare your scores with other players. The controls are designed around quick one-touch sessions with increasingly difficult level worlds.

Gameplay information is based on the current official Google Play listing.

Up Break FAQ

What is the goal in Up Break?

Smash platforms, build a high score and aim for a three-star result on every level.

Can you unlock characters?

Yes. Coins can be used to unlock different country-flag characters.

Does Up Break have leaderboards?

Yes. Google Play lists global leaderboards for comparing scores.
