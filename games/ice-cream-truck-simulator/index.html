About Ice Cream Truck Simulator

Short description

Drive a fully interactive ice cream truck, prepare orders by hand, take cash and card payments, maintain the vehicle and grow a mobile ice cream business.

Detailed description

Ice Cream Truck Simulator is a first-person food-truck simulation where the work happens directly in the 3D world. Drive through dense city traffic, find a promising place to park, refuel when needed and visit the mechanic before wear and tear interrupts a shift. The traffic system includes multiple vehicle types, reactions to obstacles and dynamic congestion.

Inside the truck, orders are prepared physically rather than through simple menus. Pick up a cone, cup or box, scoop the requested flavors and watch visible stock decrease as you serve customers. Pedestrians and special encounters can change a shift, including high-value customers, sudden crowds, thieves, food critics and a rival truck competing for business.

Reputation, tips and dynamic events add management pressure. Heatwaves can threaten the refrigeration system, points of interest can create better selling opportunities, and payments can be handled with exact cash change or the working card terminal. An in-game laptop is used to check inventory and order new supplies from the wholesaler.

What do you do in Ice Cream Truck Simulator?

Yes. Fuel use, vehicle wear and repair visits are part of operating the truck.
