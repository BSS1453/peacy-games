About Ice Cream Truck Simulator

Ice Cream Truck Simulator is a first-person business simulation where the work happens directly inside the 3D world. Drive through city traffic, choose a good selling spot, prepare cones and boxes by hand, serve customers, take cash or card payments and keep an eye on stock through the in-game laptop. The truck also needs fuel and maintenance, while customer reputation, changing events and busy city conditions can influence each shift.

Gameplay and release information is based on the current official Steam listing.

Key features

Drive and maintain the truck

Navigate city traffic, refuel at gas stations and deal with vehicle wear before it interrupts a shift.

Prepare every order by hand

Grab containers, scoop requested flavors and physically manage stock rather than relying on simple menu screens.

Serve a living city

Handle queues, pedestrians, unusual customer encounters and changing demand while choosing strong selling locations.

Manage money, inventory and reputation

Process cash and card payments, restock through the laptop and build a stronger customer reputation over time.

Ice Cream Truck Simulator FAQ

What do you do in Ice Cream Truck Simulator?

Drive the truck, choose selling spots, prepare ice cream orders, take payments, restock and maintain the business.

Does the truck need maintenance?

Yes. The official Steam listing describes fuel use, wear and repair visits as part of operating the truck.
