About Tint Trail

Tint Trail combines maze navigation with color logic. Moving through the board changes your character’s color every time, so solving a level is not simply about finding the shortest path to the exit. You need to predict how each step changes your state and choose a route that leaves you on the final tile with the required target color. The Google Play listing describes hundreds of levels, strategic route planning and a clean visual style designed to keep attention on the puzzle itself.

Tint Trail FAQ

How do you complete a Tint Trail level?

Reach the final tile while your character matches the exact target color.

Why does the route matter?

Every move changes your color, so different paths can produce different final states.
