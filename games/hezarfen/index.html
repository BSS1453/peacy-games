About Hezarfen

Short description

Fly as Hezarfen Çelebi from Galata Tower, travel as far as possible, collect coins, buy upgrades, unlock new levels and chase higher scores.

Detailed description

Hezarfen turns the legendary flight associated with Hezarfen Çelebi into a modern arcade challenge. Launch from Galata Tower, stay airborne, cover as much distance as you can and keep improving your high score while flying above the Bosphorus-inspired setting.

Coins collected during play feed directly into progression. They can be used for tools and upgrades such as a magnet, an extra life and power-ups, as well as access to additional levels that extend the challenge beyond the initial flight.

Daily login rewards, optional ad rewards and streak bonuses give you more ways to gather resources and keep upgrading future runs.
