About Demon Alive

Demon Alive is a first-person action-horror experience built around exploring an ancient forest controlled by cursed demons. Your objective is to locate the chalices hidden throughout the forest, but each discovery can change the situation: the demons can use the chalices to teleport you into dangerous domains. Progress depends on staying alive, enduring supernatural attacks and continuing the search until every chalice has been found.

Gameplay information is based on the current official Steam listing.
