About Demon Alive

Short description

Explore a demon-ruled ancient forest, recover hidden chalices, survive cursed teleportations, and fight through solo or 1–4 player co-op horror scenarios.

Detailed description

In single-player, Demon Alive sends you into a living ancient forest controlled by cursed demons. Your goal is to locate the hidden chalices while surviving the dark domains the demons can send you to. A pistol gives you a way to defend yourself and a flashlight helps you navigate the darkness, but progress depends on surviving long enough for the demons to reveal the relics you need.

The multiplayer side adds Shadow Hunt – Curse of the Chalice, a 1–4 player co-op horror mode built around finding keys and freeing a cursed chalice while different demons bring their own dangerous abilities. Light can help you see, but it can also attract certain creatures, so hiding, silence, resource use and teamwork become part of the strategy.

Demon Flood: Aksa’s Last Stand shifts the game toward co-op wave combat. Up to four players defend Aksa with weapons ranging from pistols to shotguns as increasingly difficult demon waves build toward a confrontation with the Hell Lord. The mode rewards coordinated positioning, weapon use and teamwork.
