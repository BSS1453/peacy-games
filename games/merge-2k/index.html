About Merge 2K

Short description

Swipe matching numbers into chains and merge your way toward 2048 and beyond.

Detailed description

Connect neighboring equal numbers with a swipe. Longer chains produce higher values in this colorful number puzzle.

Each level sets a target to reach within limited moves. Finish efficiently to earn up to three stars and bonus rewards.

When a board gets tricky, use Hint to find a connection, Mix to shuffle tiles or Bash to remove one. Daily rewards and streaks provide useful items for the next challenge.
