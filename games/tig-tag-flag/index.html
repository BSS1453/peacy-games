About Tig Tag Flag

Short description

A flag-powered Tic Tac Toe battle with 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boards, a tough AI opponent, private room codes and online matchmaking.

Detailed description

Tig Tag Flag reworks classic Tic Tac Toe around country flags. Choose your flag and play on 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5 boards, letting you switch between fast familiar rounds and larger layouts that require more planning.

Solo play includes a highly challenging AI opponent, while online multiplayer lets you host or join a match with a room code. Matchmaking is also available when you want the game to find another player automatically. A growing selection of flags gives each match its own visual identity.

A coin-based play system supports each match. You start with coins, can receive another free coin after the cooldown period, and can optionally use a rewarded ad to gain additional coins.
