About Tig Tag Flag

Tig Tag Flag turns classic Tic Tac Toe into a world-flag battle. Pick a flag and play on three different board sizes, from quick 3×3 rounds to larger 4×4 and 5×5 challenges. You can face the AI in single-player or play online with other people by creating or joining a match code, with matchmaking available for finding opponents automatically. The game also includes a collection of playable flags and a coin-based energy system.

Gameplay information is based on the current official Google Play listing.

Key features

Three board sizes

Switch between classic 3×3 and larger 4×4 or 5×5 boards for different levels of strategy.

Single-player AI

Practice or compete against a challenging computer opponent when you want to play solo.

Online multiplayer

Play with friends through host/join codes or use matchmaking to find an opponent.

Play with world flags

Choose from a growing selection of country flags instead of traditional X and O symbols.
