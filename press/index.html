About Peacy Games

Peacy Games is an independent game publisher and studio presence covering PC and mobile titles, with an official Steam curator/publisher presence and Google Play developer profile.

Games

  • Demon Alive — A first-person action-horror game set in an ancient forest ruled by cursed demons.
  • Ice Cream Truck Simulator — Drive a fully interactive ice cream truck and grow a hands-on food-truck business.
  • Tig Tag Flag — A flag-themed Tic Tac Toe game with multiple board sizes, AI and online multiplayer.
  • Up Break — An arcade rocket-drill game about smashing platforms, earning stars and chasing high scores.
  • Tint Trail — A color-shifting maze puzzle where every move changes your color and route planning matters.
  • Hezarfen — Fly from Galata Tower as Hezarfen Çelebi, collect coins and use upgrades to go farther.
  • Mahjong Sights — A landmark-themed Mahjong puzzle game that turns tile matching into a world tour.
